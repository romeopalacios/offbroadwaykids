Our story

Big-stage energy.
Real confidence.

At Off Broadway Kids, we’re passionate about providing children and youth with an exciting and immersive musical theater experience. Our well-rounded programs allow kids to explore the magic of live performances while also enjoying recreational activities and more.

Through dynamic musical productions and showcases, children develop confidence, creativity, and a love for the arts, all while having an unforgettable time. Whether they’re looking for the ultimate summer adventure or an engaging after-school program, Off Broadway Kids is the perfect place to shine!

Summer Spotlight

Full-Length Musical Production

Experience the thrill of a complete theatrical journey!

This summer, Off Broadway Kids proudly presents our own full-length musical production in North Hills. Students will develop skills in acting, singing, and musical theater performance, culminating in an unforgettable show day experience. This flagship program is the centerpiece of our spring offerings and a must-see opportunity for young performers.
